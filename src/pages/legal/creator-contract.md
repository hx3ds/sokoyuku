# Standard Contract Between Model User and Prototype Creator

**Effective Date:** July 21, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 21, 2026

This Standard Contract Between Model User and Prototype Creator ("Contract" or "Agreement") sets out the terms that govern each direct commercial interaction between a Model User and a Prototype Creator on the Sokoyuku platform. 

When a Model User subscribes to a Prototype Creator’s model, or consumes tokens to chat with a Prototype Creator's model (a "Model Interaction"), the Model User and the Prototype Creator enter into this legally binding Contract. This Agreement applies to the exclusion of any other terms which the Model User or Prototype Creator may propose.

**Sokoyuku Limited ("Sokoyuku," "we," "us," or "our") and its subsidiaries are not a party to this Contract.** Sokoyuku merely provides the platform, facilitates the payment, and proxies the API connections.

## 1. Parties to the Contract
*   **Prototype Creator ("Creator"):** The individual or entity that has designed, configured, and published the AI prototype and made it available as an active model on Sokoyuku.
*   **Model User ("User"):** The individual who pays a subscription fee or purchases credits to access and interact with the Creator’s model.

## 2. Grant of License and Access
Subject to the User’s payment of the applicable fees (either via a recurring subscription or per-token usage), the Creator grants the User a "Model Interaction License." 

This Model Interaction License is a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and non-sublicensable right to access and interact with the specific model via Sokoyuku’s supported third-party platforms (Telegram, Discord, Matrix).

When you interact with a Model, you may be further subject to additional terms as agreed between you and the Creator. Any such additional terms of use provided by the Creator will be linked on the prototype's details page on the Sokoyuku platform. Such terms are to be considered in addition to this document.

## 3. Ownership of Content
The Model Interaction License does not grant the User any ownership rights to the underlying model, the Creator's prototype configurations, system prompts, or intellectual property. The Creator retains all rights to their original configurations. As between the Creator and the User, the User owns all outputs generated by the model in response to the User's inputs ("Outputs"). The Creator hereby assigns to the User all its right, title, and interest (if any) in and to such Outputs, subject to the User's compliance with this Contract.

## 4. Restrictions on Use
The User agrees to the following restrictions regarding their access to the model:
1.  **Personal Use Only:** The access granted is for the User’s personal use. The User may not redistribute, resell, sublicense, or commercially exploit access to the model.
2.  **No Reverse Engineering:** The User may not attempt to reverse engineer, extract, copy, or manipulate the underlying system prompts or configurations of the Creator's prototype.
3.  **Compliance with Rules:** The User must interact with the model in strict compliance with the [Sokoyuku Terms of Service](/legal/terms), including its rules on Acceptable Use and Prohibited Conduct.

## 5. Privacy and Data Protection
The Creator agrees to adhere to the [Standard Model Privacy Policy](/legal/model-privacy) by default, or their own custom privacy policy if provided. If the Creator provides a custom privacy policy, the link to it will be available on the prototype's details page on the Sokoyuku platform. Any custom privacy policy must provide equal or greater protection for User data compared to the standard terms, with the sole exception that the Creator may use User data to train or fine-tune models if explicitly disclosed in their custom privacy policy. By default, the Creator may log, store, or monitor User data solely to ensure the model functions properly and to detect violations, and shall not use User data for training. Sokoyuku is entirely not responsible and bears no liability for the Creator's handling, storage, monitoring, or use of User data.

## 6. Pricing, Payment, and Renewals
*   **Subscriptions and Renewals:** If the model is a subscription-type, the User agrees to pay the recurring fee set by the Creator. By selecting "Subscribe," the User authorizes Sokoyuku (acting as the payment facilitator) to automatically charge the renewal fee at the end of each billing cycle unless the User cancels the subscription before the new period begins.
*   **Token Usage:** If the model is a token-type, the User agrees that their Sokoyuku credits will be deducted based on the input and output tokens consumed during the interaction.

## 7. Obligations Between Creator and User
*   **Creator Obligations:** The Creator warrants that they possess all necessary rights to grant the Model Interaction License. The Creator agrees to provide access to the model once the User has made the required payment.
*   **User Obligations:** The User agrees to make the required payment to access the model. The User assumes all risk of accessing the model unless the Creator engages in negligence or another breach of duty.
*   **Mutual Obligations:** Both the User and the Creator agree to comply at all times with the [Sokoyuku Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 8. Cancellations, Refunds, and Chargebacks
*   All payments are final. This Agreement does not grant any right to a refund unless required by mandatory statutory consumer rights laws in the User's jurisdiction.
*   The User agrees not to initiate a chargeback or reversal of payment unless the User disputes the Model Interaction in good faith. Unjustified chargebacks constitute a material breach of this Contract.

## 9. Expiry and Termination of License
The Model Interaction License expires automatically and without notice if:
1.  The payment related to the Model Interaction was unsuccessful, charged back, or reversed.
2.  An active Subscription period ends and is not renewed.
3.  The User's Sokoyuku account runs out of credits (for token-type models).
4.  The Creator deletes the model, prototype, or their Creator account.
5.  The User's or Creator's Sokoyuku account is suspended or terminated for breaching the [Sokoyuku Terms of Service](/legal/terms).
6.  The User closes their account.

## 10. Disclaimers and No Guarantees
*   **AI Outputs:** The User acknowledges that the model is powered by generative artificial intelligence. The outputs generated by the model are probabilistic and may be inaccurate, inappropriate, or incomplete. 
*   **Platform Availability:** The User acknowledges that circumstances may prevent access to the model, including if the availability of all or any part of Sokoyuku or third-party platforms (Telegram, Discord, Matrix) is suspended or inaccessible.
*   **No Warranties:** The Creator provides access to the model "AS IS" and makes no warranties regarding the accuracy, reliability, or suitability of the model for any particular purpose.
*   **Limitation of Liability:** To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Creator shall not be liable to the User for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the User's reliance on or use of the model's outputs.

## 11. Governing Law and Severability
*   **Governing Law:** To the greatest extent permitted by the laws of the place where the User lives, this Agreement is governed by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China (aligning with Sokoyuku's jurisdiction), which will apply to any claim that arises out of or relates to this Agreement. 
*   **Severability:** In the event any provision of this Agreement is found by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
