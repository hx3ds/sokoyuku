# Standard Contract Between Model User and Prototype Creator

**Effective Date:** July 21, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 3, 2026

This Standard Contract Between Model User and Prototype Creator ("Contract" or "Agreement") sets out the terms that govern each direct commercial interaction between a Model User and a Prototype Creator on the Sokoyuku platform. 

When a Model User pays for a period of access to a Prototype Creator’s model, or uses a period of access the Creator offers without a charge (a "Model Interaction"), the Model User and the Prototype Creator enter into this legally binding Contract. This Agreement applies to the exclusion of any other terms which the Model User or Prototype Creator may propose.

**Sokoyuku Limited ("Sokoyuku," "we," "us," or "our") and its subsidiaries are not a party to this Contract.** Sokoyuku is a SaaS platform that allows and facilitates the User to use a model hosted by the Creator in a supported third-party messaging app. The User may use Sokoyuku to manage accounts or bots of those apps and to add and chat with the model. The Creator uses Sokoyuku to enable messaging between those accounts or bots and the model. Sokoyuku also facilitates a one-time Stripe direct charge so the User pays the Creator. The Creator is the merchant of record. Sokoyuku's take is an application fee only. The charge does not auto-renew and does not save the card. Creators are responsible for all Stripe fees on the connected account, including processor fees, foreign-exchange fees, payout fees, and dispute fees. Sokoyuku is not responsible for disputes or chargebacks on those payments.

## 1. Parties to the Contract

- **Prototype Creator ("Creator"):** The individual or entity that has designed, configured, and published the prototype, hosts the model, and uses Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and models.
- **Model User ("User"):** The individual who uses Sokoyuku to add and chat with the Creator's model in a supported third-party messaging app, including a person who pays for a period of access or uses a period of access the Creator offers without a charge.



## 2. Grant of License and Access

Subject to the User’s payment of the applicable fee, or to a period of access the Creator offers without a charge, the Creator grants the User a "Model Interaction License." 

This Model Interaction License is a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and non-sublicensable right to use the specific model, hosted by the Creator, in supported third-party messaging apps (including Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, and WhatsApp) and, when the Creator enables calls, by a voice call in the Sokoyuku app. The User may use Sokoyuku to manage the account or bot and to add and chat with the model. The Creator uses Sokoyuku to enable messaging between that account or bot and the model.

When you interact with a Model, you may be further subject to additional terms as agreed between you and the Creator. Any such additional terms of use provided by the Creator will be linked on the prototype's details page on the Sokoyuku platform. Such terms are to be considered in addition to this document.

## 3. Ownership of Content

The license does not give the User ownership of the Creator's model or of the prototype listing. The Creator keeps those. As between the Creator and the User, the User owns the outputs the model generates in response to the User's inputs ("Outputs"). The Creator assigns those Outputs to the User, subject to this Contract. Sokoyuku does not generate the Outputs.

## 4. Restrictions on Use

The User agrees to the following restrictions regarding their access to the model:

1. **Personal Use Only:** The access granted is for the User’s personal use. The User may not redistribute, resell, sublicense, or commercially exploit access to the model.
2. **No Reverse Engineering:** The User may not attempt to reverse engineer, extract, copy, or manipulate the underlying system prompts or configurations of the Creator's prototype.
3. **Compliance with Rules:** The User must interact with the model in strict compliance with the [Sokoyuku Terms of Service](/legal/terms), including its rules on Acceptable Use and Prohibited Conduct.



## 5. Privacy and Data Protection

The Creator agrees to adhere to the [Standard Model Privacy Policy](/legal/model-privacy) by default, or their own custom privacy policy if provided. If the Creator provides a custom privacy policy, the link to it will be available on the prototype's details page on the Sokoyuku platform. Any custom privacy policy must provide equal or greater protection for User data compared to the standard terms, with the sole exception that the Creator may use User data to train or fine-tune models if explicitly disclosed in their custom privacy policy. By default, the Creator may log, store, or monitor User data solely to ensure the model functions properly and to detect violations, and shall not use User data for training. Sokoyuku is not responsible and bears no liability for the Creator's handling, storage, monitoring, or use of User data.

## 6. Pricing and Payment

- **One-time charge:** The User pays the fee the Creator sets for one or more periods, in the currency shown at checkout. A prototype may offer a free tier and paid tiers. The tier, price, and interval are shown when the User pays. The charge is paid directly to the Creator. Sokoyuku's take is an application fee. The charge does not auto-renew and does not save the card. Access lasts until the end date Sokoyuku records and does not continue on its own. Free access, where offered, lasts only for the period granted. The User may pay again while access is still running. The interval is daily or monthly.
- **Creator Stripe fees:** The Creator pays Stripe's fees. Sokoyuku does not charge a fee for receiving model payments. A platform subscription is a separate, optional plan.
- **Price changes:** The Creator sets the price. A price change does not change access already paid for. The new price applies to the next charge.
- **Taxes:** Prices may be shown exclusive of tax. The Creator remits tax collected on model sales.
- **Refunds:** The Creator may refund a model payment from the Stripe Dashboard. A succeeded refund, including a partial refund, shortens the access that charge paid for. A partial refund shortens it in proportion to the amount refunded.
- **Stripe account unavailable:** If the Creator's Stripe account is missing, restricted, closed, or disconnected, or tax collection is not ready, the paid prototype is hidden, new models cannot be added to it, and a new model payment cannot be made. Access already paid for continues until it ends, or until a refund or lost dispute shortens it.
- **Immediate digital performance:** The Model Interaction is digital content supplied with the User's consent to begin performance immediately upon successful payment. Where permitted by law, the User acknowledges that the statutory right to cancel distance contracts for digital content may not apply once performance has begun. Mandatory consumer rights that cannot be waived remain unaffected.
- **Payment support:** The Creator must provide a reasonable contact method for payment and access issues related to the Model Interaction (for example, an email or support link on the prototype page).



## 7. Obligations Between Creator and User

- **Creator Obligations:** The Creator warrants that they possess all necessary rights to grant the Model Interaction License. The Creator agrees to provide access for a paid period once the payment succeeds, and for a free period when that period is granted.
- **User Obligations:** Where a fee applies, the User agrees to pay it. The User assumes all risk of accessing the model unless the Creator engages in negligence or another breach of duty.
- **Mutual Obligations:** Both the User and the Creator agree to comply at all times with the [Sokoyuku Terms of Service](/legal/terms).



## 8. Cancellations, Refunds, and Chargebacks

- All payments are final. This Agreement does not grant any right to a refund unless required by mandatory statutory consumer rights in the User's jurisdiction, or unless the Creator refunds the charge as described in Section 6.
- The User agrees not to initiate a chargeback or reversal of payment unless the User disputes the Model Interaction in good faith. Unjustified chargebacks constitute a material breach of this Contract.
- Disputes and chargebacks on model payments, and the related fees, are between the User, the Creator, and Stripe on the Creator's connected account. Sokoyuku is not responsible for those disputes. A lost dispute shortens the access that charge paid for. If that dispute is later won, that access is restored. A succeeded refund shortens that access as stated in Section 6.



## 9. Expiry and Termination of License

The Model Interaction License lasts until the model access period end. It expires, in whole or for the time no longer covered, if:

1. The charge does not succeed. No access is granted for that charge.
2. The paid period ends. It does not continue on its own.
3. A succeeded refund or a lost dispute shortens the access that charge paid for. Access still covered by another charge continues. A dispute that is later won restores the access it removed.
4. The Creator removes the prototype when no paid access is still running.
5. The User's or Creator's Sokoyuku account is suspended, terminated, or closed.

Hiding a prototype because the Creator's Stripe account is missing, restricted, closed, or disconnected, or because tax collection is not ready, does not end a period already granted.
