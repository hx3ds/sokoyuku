# Standard Model Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 3, 2026

**Last Updated:** October 3, 2026

The following serves as a standard Privacy Policy for models hosted by creators on the Sokoyuku SaaS platform. Sokoyuku allows and facilitates users to use those models in supported third-party messaging apps. Users can use Sokoyuku to manage accounts or bots of those apps and to add and chat with models. Creators can use Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and models. By design, this policy is written to be generally applicable to a wide range of models. It sets out the privacy terms between Prototype Creators and Model Users. Creators should add further privacy disclosures if their models or third-party integrations require them.

## 1. Terms and Definitions
1.1. **Sokoyuku** – Sokoyuku Limited.

1.2. **Platform** – The Sokoyuku SaaS platform.

1.3. **Creator** – The person or legal entity who designs, configures, and publishes the prototype, hosts the Model, and uses Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and the Model.

1.4. **Model** – An instance of a prototype hosted by the Creator. Sokoyuku does not host the Model.

1.5. **User** – The person who uses Sokoyuku to manage an account or bot of a supported third-party messaging app and to add and chat with the Model. Supported apps include, without limitation, Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, and WhatsApp. When the Creator enables calls, the User may also place a voice call in the Sokoyuku app.

1.6. **Policy** – This document, governing the privacy relationship between the Creator and the User.

## 2. General Provisions
2.1. This Policy is a standard document that applies to all models and prototypes on the Platform by default, unless the Creator has published a separate, custom privacy policy. Any such custom privacy policy provided by the Creator will be linked on the prototype's details page on the Sokoyuku platform.

2.2. If a Creator provides a custom privacy policy, it shall supersede this Policy only to the extent that it provides equal or greater protection for User data, with one exception: a Creator may specify in their custom policy that they collect and use User data for model training. Any such deviation regarding model training must be explicitly and prominently disclosed in the Creator's custom privacy policy.

2.3. This Policy governs solely the relationship between the Creator and the User. It cannot and does not regulate the relationship between Sokoyuku and its users, nor does it supersede the [Sokoyuku Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

2.4. This Policy regulates the collection, storage, distribution, usage, and protection of information of Users who access the Model.

2.5. Your continued access to and use of the Model shall constitute your acceptance of this Policy and the [Standard Contract Between Model User and Prototype Creator](/legal/creator-contract).

## 3. Disclaimers
3.1. The Model is hosted by the Creator. It is not maintained or endorsed by Sokoyuku, nor is it affiliated with Sokoyuku. Sokoyuku allows the User to add and chat with the Model in supported third-party messaging apps, and facilitates that use. The Creator uses Sokoyuku to enable messaging between the User's account or bot and the Model.

3.2. The User agrees to use the Model only as permitted by applicable law.

## 4. Collection and Processing of Data
4.1. The Creator, through the Model, requests, collects, and processes only the data necessary for the Model's features to function.

4.2. **Data Processing and Monitoring:** The Creator may log, store, or monitor the User's inputs and the Model's outputs solely to the extent necessary to provide the Model's core functionality, maintain system security, and detect violations of applicable rules or Terms of Service. Any data stored for these purposes must be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill these operational requirements.

4.3. **No Sokoyuku Liability:** Sokoyuku is not responsible and bears no liability for the Creator's logging, storage, monitoring, or any potential mishandling of User data.

4.4. **No Training by Default:** By default, the Creator shall not use the User's inputs or outputs to train or fine-tune any models. The Creator may only use User data for training purposes if they provide a custom privacy policy that explicitly discloses this practice and supersedes this clause.

4.5. **Third-Party APIs:** The Creator must ensure any third-party APIs used by the prototype are configured not to use User data for training, unless explicitly disclosed otherwise in the Creator's custom privacy policy.

4.6. **No Secondary Monetization:** The Creator will not monetize User data or use it for any purpose other than the purposes described in this Policy.

4.7. **No Third-Party Transfers:** Private User information will not be transferred or made accessible to any third party, except as stipulated by this Policy or required by law.

## 5. Data Protection
5.1. The Creator will use appropriate measures to protect the integrity and confidentiality of the data the Creator processes.

5.2. The Creator will handle, transfer, and store User information in compliance with applicable law, and will take precautions against unauthorized access, modification, deletion, or distribution.

## 6. Rights and Obligations
6.1. The Creator hosts the Model. It runs at the address the Creator supplies. Sokoyuku does not host the Model, does not generate the Model's outputs, and does not keep message content (which is a message's text, caption, or attachments), but may keep metadata about messages. Sokoyuku enables messaging between the User's account or bot and the Model so the User can chat with the Model, and may relay those messages. If the Creator enables calls, Sokoyuku may relay the call signaling and the call media, and does not keep a recording of the call. Sokoyuku may end the connection or remove the Model from the Service if either party abuses the Platform.

6.2. The Creator shall comply with the stipulations set forth in this Policy. If the Creator logs or stores User data as permitted under Section 4.2, the Creator must provide an easily accessible avenue (e.g., a contact email, a support channel, or an automated command within the Model) for Users to exercise their rights to access, amend, or delete their data under applicable law.

6.3. The User may exercise the right to amend their data, to restrict or object to the processing of their data, or to withdraw consent at any time, including by stopping use of the Model.
