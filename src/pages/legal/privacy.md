## 1. Introduction

Sokoyuku Limited ("Sokoyuku," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and we are committed to protecting the Personal Data we process about you. Sokoyuku Limited is a company based in Hong Kong and is the owner and operator of Sokoyuku, a web service that allows users to create AI prototypes, interact with models (instances of prototypes), and connect Telegram bots, Discord bots, or Matrix accounts to proxy AI interactions.

This privacy policy ("Policy") explains our practices with respect to the Personal Data processed about our users, including "Prototype Creators" (users who create prototypes) and "Model Users" (users who interact with models or subscribe to them).

We process your Personal Data when you use Sokoyuku and for the provision of the services that we offer via Sokoyuku (the "Services"). We act as a "data controller" of the Personal Data that we process in connection with the Services. This means that we decide the reasons why we process Personal Data about you and how we do so. 

## 2. What is Personal Data?

"Personal Data" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular person or household.

We may also collect data that is not capable of identifying you or is otherwise not associated or linked with you, such as deidentified, aggregated, or anonymised information. This type of data is not Personal Data and our use of such data is not subject to this Policy.

## 3. Applicability of this Policy (18+)

This Policy is provided in addition to, but does not form part of, our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) that govern your use of Sokoyuku and the Services.

Our Services are strictly intended for individuals 18 years of age or older. Anyone under 18 years of age is not permitted to register, access, or use the Services. By using the Services, you represent that you are at least 18 years of age. If we learn that we have collected information from or about a person under the age of 18, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.

## 4. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We process, or our third-party providers process on our behalf, different kinds of Personal Data about Prototype Creators and Model Users, which we have grouped together as follows:

### 4.1 User and Account Data
- **Identifiers:** Full name, alias or username, email address, and account password. If you choose to register or sign in using a third-party authentication provider (e.g., Google or Telegram), we may receive and collect data provided by that service, such as your user ID, email address, display name, and profile picture.
- **Bot and Connected Account Credentials:** API keys, access tokens, and authentication credentials uploaded by you to connect your Telegram bots, Discord bots, or Matrix accounts ("Connected Accounts"). **You must be the owner of the Connected Accounts you link to Sokoyuku.**
- **Local Conductor Data:** If you utilize a Local Conductor, your local account tokens and local model settings are encrypted client-side using your public key before being uploaded. Sokoyuku stores this data only in its encrypted form and cannot access the plaintext.
- **Profile Data:** Avatars, headers, prototypes you create, models you add, and your platform or model subscriptions.
- **Support Data:** Customer support queries, feedback, and communications you submit to us.

### 4.2 Financial and Transaction Data
Sokoyuku uses third-party payment processors to handle all transactions. Depending on whether you are purchasing credits, subscribing to models/platforms, or receiving payouts as a Prototype Creator, we or our payment processors may collect:
- **Payment Information:** Payment card details (processed via third-party processors; we do not receive your full payment card number), billing address, and funds added to your account (credits).
- **Transaction History:** Records of platform subscriptions, model subscriptions, token usage payments, and credit purchases.
- **Payout Information (Prototype Creators):** Bank account information, pay-out country, corporate or business entity name, identification numbers, tax identification numbers, and related tax forms (e.g., W-9, 1099, VAT numbers) necessary to remit revenue to you after platform fees are deducted.

### 4.3 Technical and Network Data
Internet or other electronic network activity information, including:
- Internet protocol (IP) address (and country code).
- User agent, browser type and version, time zone setting, and operating system.
- Device identifiers and connection information.

### 4.4 Usage Data
- Information about your use of the Services, including timestamps of logins, features accessed, search queries, and interaction metrics with prototypes and models.

### 4.5 Message Meta Data
- We collect routing and transmission metadata concerning the messages sent and received via your Connected Accounts to properly proxy requests to the assigned models. **(See Section 7 for critical information regarding message content privacy).**

## 7. Message Content Privacy and Data Relaying

Sokoyuku operates as a relay and proxy service bridging your Connected Accounts (Telegram, Discord, Matrix) and AI models. We have strict data limitations regarding message contents:

- **No Storage of User Message Content:** Sokoyuku **does not store** any message contents received from users' Connected Accounts. 
- **No Storage of Model Message Content:** Sokoyuku **does not store** any message contents, outputs, or action requests generated by or received from models.
- **Real-Time Relaying:** We receive messages from connected accounts and instantly relay them to the assigned models, and vice versa via the accounts' platform APIs.
- **Meta Data Only:** We only store meta data about messages (e.g., timestamps, token counts, routing identifiers) necessary for billing token usage, rate-limiting, and maintaining the technical stability of the proxy service.
- **Local Conductors:** If you choose to host a Local Conductor, message routing for your "local accounts" and "local models" occurs entirely on your own infrastructure. For these local components, Sokoyuku's cloud servers do not proxy, receive, or process your message contents or routing metadata. The security of data processed by a Local Conductor is solely your responsibility.
