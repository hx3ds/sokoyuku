## 1. Introduction and Data Controller

Sokoyuku Limited ("Sokoyuku," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the Personal Data we process about you. 

Sokoyuku Limited is a company incorporated in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China and is the owner and operator of Sokoyuku. Sokoyuku is a SaaS platform that allows and facilitates users to use models hosted by creators in supported third-party messaging apps. Users can use Sokoyuku to manage accounts or bots of those apps and to add and chat with models. Creators can use Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and models. Supported apps include, without limitation, Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, and WhatsApp. If calls are enabled, a user may place a voice call in the Sokoyuku app. Creators host the models. Sokoyuku does not host them. Messaging that Sokoyuku enables between users' accounts or bots and models may be relayed by Sokoyuku. Sokoyuku does not keep message content (which is a message's text, caption, or attachments), but may keep metadata about messages.

This privacy policy ("Policy") explains our practices with respect to the Personal Data processed about our users, including:
- **Prototype Creators:** Users who publish prototypes, host the models made from them, and use Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and models;
- **Model Users:** Users who use Sokoyuku to add and chat with those models in supported third-party messaging apps, or who pay for a period of access to them; and
- **Third-Party Messaging Participants:** Individuals who communicate with a connected bot or account via third-party messaging apps without registering an account on Sokoyuku (see Section 8).

We act as a "data controller" (or "data user" under the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance) of the Personal Data that we process in connection with the Services. This means that we determine the purposes and means of processing Personal Data about you.

## 2. What is Personal Data?

"Personal Data" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular living individual or household. Under the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO), it means any data relating directly or indirectly to a living individual from which it is practicable for the identity of the individual to be directly or indirectly ascertained.

We may also collect data that is not capable of identifying you or is otherwise not associated or linked with you, such as deidentified, aggregated, or anonymized technical information. This type of data is not Personal Data, and our use of such data is not subject to this Policy.

## 3. Applicability of this Policy (18+)

This Policy is provided for informational transparency regarding our privacy practices and does not form part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

Our Services are strictly intended for individuals who are at least eighteen (18) years of age, or the age of legal majority where they live, whichever is higher. Anyone who does not meet that age is not permitted to register, access, or use the Services. By using the Services, you represent that you meet this age requirement. If we learn that we have collected information from or about a person who does not, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.

## 4. Categories of Personal Data We Collect (Obligatory vs. Voluntary)

Under Data Protection Principle 1(3) of the Hong Kong PDPO and applicable data protection laws, we inform you whether it is obligatory or voluntary for you to supply each category of Personal Data, and the consequences of failing to supply it:

### 4.1 User and Account Data
- **Identifiers (Obligatory for Registration):** Name, username, email address, and password. The password is stored only as a cryptographic hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email, name, and account identifier Google sends. If you sign in with Telegram, we receive the identifier and name Telegram sends. Telegram does not provide an email address.
  - *Consequence:* Supplying this data is obligatory to register, authenticate, and maintain your account. If not provided, you cannot register or access the Service.
- **Messaging Account Credentials (Obligatory for Non-Local Connected Accounts):** Credentials you upload so Sokoyuku can manage a Messaging Account for you on supported third-party messaging apps (Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, WhatsApp). Credentials for a local account running on your own station are encrypted client-side before upload; we cannot read them or recover them if you lose your key. Credentials for remote managed accounts or bots are stored to operate that account or bot.
  - *Consequence:* Supplying these credentials is obligatory if you choose to connect a remote Messaging Account. If not provided, Sokoyuku cannot manage that bot or route its messages.
- **Profile and Prototype Data (Voluntary):** Prototype titles, descriptions, settings, and visibility preferences you configure.
  - *Consequence:* Configurable at your discretion; default values are applied if omitted.
- **Email Verification Codes (Obligatory for Sign-up/Password Reset):** Short-lived verification codes sent to your email.
  - *Consequence:* Required to verify account ownership during registration or password recovery.
- **Support Communications (Voluntary):** Queries, feedback, and correspondence you submit to customer support.
  - *Consequence:* Voluntary; if not provided, we may be unable to investigate or resolve your specific support inquiry.

### 4.2 Financial and Transaction Data
Sokoyuku uses Stripe to process transactions. Depending on whether you purchase a platform subscription, pay a Creator for model access, or receive payouts as a Creator:
- **Payment Information (Obligatory for Paid Services):** Stripe processes your payment card or payment method. Sokoyuku does not store your full card number. A model payment is a one-time direct charge that does not save your card. A platform subscription may maintain a payment method with Stripe for renewals.
  - *Consequence:* Required to execute payment. If not provided, paid access cannot be granted.
- **Transaction Records (Obligatory):** Records of platform subscriptions, model payments, granted access periods, and Sokoyuku application fees.
  - *Consequence:* Required for tax compliance, accounting, dispute handling, and fulfilling the contract.
- **Creator Stripe Connect Account (Obligatory for Paid Creators):** Link to the Creator's Stripe Connect account. Stripe directly collects identity, banking, KYC, and tax details in Stripe-hosted onboarding.
  - *Consequence:* Supplying this information to Stripe is obligatory for Creators wishing to publish paid prototypes. If omitted, the prototype cannot accept payments and remains hidden.

### 4.3 Technical, Network, and Server Log Data
- **Server Logs (Automatically Collected):** IP address, request timestamp, HTTP status, and API path requested.
  - *Consequence:* Collected automatically to ensure server availability, rate limiting, and network security.

### 4.4 Usage Data
- Sign-in timestamps, API path requests, and search query terms entered in the prototype directory (processed transiently to display results; search history is not logged).

### 4.5 Messages and Relaying Metadata
- When you chat with a model, Sokoyuku relays messages between the Messaging Account and the model hosted by the Creator. We do not store message content (text, captions, or attachments). We store operational metadata (account ID, chat ID, messaging-app user ID, and timestamp) for a limited period (by default, 30 days) to operate routing and platform security. **(See Section 7 and Section 8.)**

### 4.6 Voice Calls
- When a prototype enables calls, you may place a voice call in the Sokoyuku app. We store call session metadata (model, account, chat, start and end timestamps) and WebRTC signaling necessary to connect and terminate the call. Call media may be relayed via TURN servers. We do not record or store call audio. The call session key remains in your local browser storage.

## 5. How We Obtain Your Personal Data

We obtain Personal Data from the following sources:
- **Directly from you:** When you register, upload credentials, publish prototypes, purchase access, or contact support.
- **From third-party authentication providers:** When you choose to authenticate via Google or Telegram.
- **Automatically from your device:** Technical and network data collected via server logs and the session cookie when accessing the Service.
- **From third-party messaging platforms:** Incoming and outgoing routing metadata received via Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, or WhatsApp APIs.
- **From service providers:** Transaction confirmations and Connect account status from Stripe; anti-bot verification from Cloudflare Turnstile.

## 7. Message Content Privacy and Data Relaying

Sokoyuku enables messaging between Messaging Accounts and models hosted by Creators so users can chat with models. 
- **No Content Storage:** Sokoyuku does not host the models, does not generate model outputs, and does not store message content (text, photos, files, or attachments). Message contents pass through transiently in memory for routing and relaying.
- **Metadata Storage:** We store operational metadata (messaging app account ID, chat ID, user ID, and timestamp) for a strictly limited retention period (by default, 30 days) to troubleshoot transmission errors, prevent duplicate delivery, and enforce rate limits.
- **Local Stations:** For local models hosted on your own station, message contents stay on your local host. You are solely responsible for securing that host.
- **Voice Calls:** We store call signaling and session metadata to establish the WebRTC connection. We do not record or retain audio streams.

## 8. Messaging Participants Who Are Not Registered Users (GDPR Art. 14 Disclosures)

When an account holder uses Sokoyuku to connect a bot or Messaging Account on a third-party messaging platform (such as Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, or WhatsApp), individuals on those platforms may interact with the bot without having an account on Sokoyuku. Pursuant to Article 14 of the GDPR and UK GDPR (processing data not obtained directly from the data subject):
- **Categories of Data Processed:** Third-party messaging platform user ID, chat ID, timestamp, and message routing metadata.
- **Source of Data:** Received directly from the relevant third-party messaging platform API as initiated by the messaging participant.
- **No Content Storage:** Sokoyuku does not store the text or attachments of messages sent by messaging participants.
- **Purpose and Lawful Basis:** Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / UK GDPR) and the legitimate interests of the account holder to route and relay incoming messages to the designated model, return generated replies to the messaging app, prevent system spam, and maintain platform security.
- **Retention:** Routing metadata is retained for up to 30 days and then automatically deleted.
- **Rights of Non-Registered Participants:** Messaging participants have the right under GDPR and UK GDPR to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of their metadata, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not maintain accounts or identities for messaging participants, any request must include the specific third-party messaging platform, user ID/handle, and relevant chat identifier so that we may locate any matching metadata. Requests may be sent to [privacy@sokoyuku.com](mailto:privacy@sokoyuku.com).

## 9. Sharing and Disclosing Your Personal Data

We may share your Personal Data with the following categories of recipients:

Sokoyuku does not provide the Creator with your payment card number.
- **Service Providers (Processors):** Trusted third-party vendors who provide infrastructure, payment processing, email delivery, and security services on our behalf (e.g., Stripe for payment processing; Cloudflare for CDN, Turnstile verification, and DDoS protection). These providers process data under strict contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations.
- **Corporate Successors:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of company assets, user data may be transferred to the acquiring entity subject to this Policy.
- **Law Enforcement and Legal Process:** If required by applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority, or where necessary in good faith to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sokoyuku, our users, or the public.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Sokoyuku Limited is incorporated and headquartered in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China. Our infrastructure and service providers operate servers globally, including in the European Economic Area, the United States, and Hong Kong.

- **Absence of an Adequacy Decision:** The European Commission and UK authorities have not issued a general adequacy decision for Hong Kong or the United States.
- **Safeguards Relied Upon:** Where Personal Data originating from the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland is transferred to Hong Kong or other third countries lacking an adequacy decision, we ensure an adequate level of data protection by implementing standard contractual safeguards:
  - For EEA transfers: The European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914);
  - For UK transfers: The **UK International Data Transfer Addendum** to the EU SCCs or the International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA);
  - Supplementary technical, organizational, and encryption measures to safeguard data against unauthorized access.
- **Obtaining a Copy:** You may obtain a copy of the applicable transfer safeguards by contacting us at [privacy@sokoyuku.com](mailto:privacy@sokoyuku.com).

## 11. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or to satisfy legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements:
- **Account Data:** Retained for the active lifetime of your account. Upon account deletion, identifiers and credentials are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.
- **Financial & Transaction Records:** Retained for 7 to 10 years to satisfy Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department requirements, statutory tax rules, and accounting standards.
- **Message Routing Metadata:** Retained for a maximum of 30 days and automatically deleted thereafter.
- **Voice Call Signaling:** Retained for the duration of the session and up to 30 days for operational diagnostics; audio streams are not recorded.

## 12. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, destruction, alteration, or disclosure. Local Messaging Account credentials and local model settings are encrypted before upload using keys that Sokoyuku cannot read or recover. Transmissions are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

## 13. Your Rights Regarding Personal Data (Hong Kong PDPO, GDPR, and UK DPA)

### 13.1 Rights Under the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO)
If your data is subject to the Hong Kong PDPO, you have the statutory right under Data Protection Principle 6 and Part 5 of the PDPO to:
- Ascertain whether Sokoyuku Limited holds personal data relating to you;
- Request a copy of such personal data within a reasonable time and for a fee that is not excessive;
- Request the correction of any personal data that is inaccurate.

### 13.2 Rights Under GDPR and UK GDPR
If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR:
- **Right of Access (Art. 15):** Request confirmation of whether we process your data and obtain a copy of it.
- **Right to Rectification (Art. 16):** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure / Deletion (Art. 17):** Request erasure of your data where retention is no longer necessary.
- **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18):** Request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances.
- **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20):** Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)):** Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

### 13.3 How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise any of your rights under the PDPO, GDPR, UK GDPR, or other privacy laws, contact our Privacy Contact at:
- **Email:** [privacy@sokoyuku.com](mailto:privacy@sokoyuku.com)
- **Postal Address:** Privacy Contact, Sokoyuku Limited, Hong Kong.

We will verify your identity before processing your request. We respond to valid requests within the statutory timeframes (within 40 calendar days under the Hong Kong PDPO; within one month under the GDPR / UK GDPR).

### 13.4 Right to Lodge a Complaint with a Supervisory Authority
You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority:
- **In Hong Kong:** Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD), Hong Kong (Website: [www.pcpd.org.hk](https://www.pcpd.org.hk)).
- **In the European Union:** The national supervisory authority in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of alleged infringement.
- **In the United Kingdom:** The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF (Website: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).
