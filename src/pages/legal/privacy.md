## 1. Introduction

Sokoyuku Limited ("Sokoyuku," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and we are committed to protecting the Personal Data we process about you. Sokoyuku Limited is a company based in Hong Kong and is the owner and operator of Sokoyuku. Sokoyuku is a SaaS platform that allows and facilitates users to use models hosted by creators in supported third-party messaging apps. Users can use Sokoyuku to manage accounts or bots of those apps and add and chat with models. Creators can use Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and models. Supported apps include Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, and WhatsApp, and are not limited to those apps. When a prototype allows it, a user may place a voice call in the Sokoyuku app. Creators host the models. Sokoyuku does not. Messaging Sokoyuku enables between users' accounts or bots and models may be relayed by Sokoyuku. Sokoyuku does not keep the message text.

This privacy policy ("Policy") explains our practices with respect to the Personal Data processed about our users, including "Prototype Creators" (users who host prototypes and use Sokoyuku to enable messaging between users' accounts or bots and models) and "Model Users" (users who use Sokoyuku to add and chat with those models in supported third-party messaging apps, or who pay for a period of access to them).

We process your Personal Data when you use Sokoyuku and for the provision of the services that we offer via Sokoyuku (the "Services"). We act as a "data controller" of the Personal Data that we process in connection with the Services. This means that we decide the reasons why we process Personal Data about you and how we do so. 

## 2. What is Personal Data?

"Personal Data" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular person or household.

We may also collect data that is not capable of identifying you or is otherwise not associated or linked with you, such as deidentified, aggregated, or anonymised information. This type of data is not Personal Data and our use of such data is not subject to this Policy.

## 3. Applicability of this Policy (18+)

This Policy is provided in addition to, but does not form part of, our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) that govern your use of Sokoyuku and the Services.

Our Services are strictly intended for individuals who are at least eighteen (18) years of age, or the age of legal majority where they live, whichever is higher. Anyone who does not meet that age is not permitted to register, access, or use the Services. By using the Services, you represent that you meet this age requirement. If we learn that we have collected information from or about a person who does not, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.

## 4. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We process, or our third-party providers process on our behalf, different kinds of Personal Data about Prototype Creators and Model Users, which we have grouped together as follows:

### 4.1 User and Account Data
- **Identifiers:** Name, username, email address, and password. The password is stored only as a hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email, name, and account identifier Google sends. If you sign in with Telegram, we receive the identifier and name Telegram sends. Telegram does not provide an email address.
- **Messaging Account credentials:** The credentials you upload so Sokoyuku can manage a Messaging Account for you. Messaging Accounts are accounts or bots on supported third-party messaging apps, including Telegram, Discord, Matrix, QQ, and WhatsApp, and are not limited to those apps. You must own the account or bot you manage. Credentials for a local account are encrypted before upload. We cannot read them or recover them if you lose your key. Credentials for an account or bot you use Sokoyuku to manage, other than a local account, are stored so the Service can manage that account or bot.
- **Profile:** Prototypes and models you create, your platform subscription, and the model access you have paid for.
- **Email verification:** A short-lived code sent to your email for sign-up or a password change.
- **Support Data:** Customer support queries, feedback, and communications you submit to us.

### 4.2 Financial and Transaction Data
Sokoyuku uses Stripe to handle payments. Depending on whether you buy a platform subscription, pay a Creator for a period of model access, or receive those payments as a Prototype Creator, we or Stripe may collect:
- **Payment information:** Stripe processes the card. We do not store your card number. A model payment does not save the card. A platform subscription may keep a payment method with Stripe so it can renew.
- **Transaction records:** Platform subscriptions, model payments, the model access period each payment grants, and Sokoyuku's application fee. Model payment principal stays on the connected account and never settles on Sokoyuku's balance.
- **Creator connected account:** We keep the link to the Creator's connected account. Stripe holds the bank, identity, and tax details.

### 4.3 Technical and Network Data
- IP address in server logs, and the API path requested.

### 4.4 Usage Data
- When you sign in, and which API paths you request, as recorded in server logs. We use prototype search text to return results. We do not keep a history of that search text.

### 4.5 Messages
- Sokoyuku may relay messages between a Messaging Account and a model hosted by a Creator when a user chats with that model. We do not keep the message text. We may keep limited records needed to operate the Service, such as which account, chat, and messaging-app user a message belonged to, and when. Those records are kept for a limited time, by default 30 days. **(See Section 7.)**

### 4.6 Voice calls
- If a prototype allows calls, you can place a voice call in the Sokoyuku app. We store the call session (which model, account, and chat it belongs to, and when it opened and closed) and the signaling needed to connect and end the call. Call media may be relayed through a TURN server. We do not keep a recording of the call. The call session key stays in your browser's local storage.

## 5. How We Obtain Your Personal Data

We collect your Personal Data from the following categories of sources:
- **Directly from you:** When you register for an account, manage an account or bot, create a prototype, make a purchase, or contact support.
- **From third-party authentication providers:** When you choose to sign in or register using a third-party service like Google or Telegram.
- **Automatically or indirectly from you:** Through your use of the Services (technical data, usage data, message records, and call records) via the sign-in cookie and server logs.
- **From our service providers:** Such as Stripe, which sends us payment confirmations, and Cloudflare, which verifies the Turnstile check on sign-up and password change.

## 7. Message Content Privacy and Data Relaying

Sokoyuku enables messaging between your Messaging Accounts and a model hosted by a Creator, so you can chat with that model. Sokoyuku may relay those messages. Sokoyuku does not host the model, does not generate the model's output, and does not keep the message text. The limited operational records in Section 4.5 are kept for a limited time, by default 30 days.

For a local model, the message text stays on your host. You are responsible for that host. We may still keep the limited operational records described in Section 4.5.

If you place a voice call, we keep the call session and signaling described in Section 4.6. We do not keep a recording of the call.

## 8. Sharing and Disclosing Your Personal Data

We may share your Personal Data with the following categories of third parties:

- **Between Users:** When Model Users pay for access to or interact with models, certain necessary transaction and usage data is shared with the Prototype Creator to fulfill the contract between the two parties. The model payment is a direct charge on the Creator's connected account. Sokoyuku's take is an application fee. Sokoyuku does not pass the Creator the User's full card number.
- **Service Providers:** We share data with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our business, including payment processing providers, cloud hosting, email delivery, and IT security. These providers act as our data processors and are contractually bound to keep your data secure.
- **Corporate Transactions:** To a buyer or successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, reorganization, or sale of assets.
- **Law Enforcement and Authorities:** We may disclose your Personal Data if required to do so by law, or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect and defend the rights or property of Sokoyuku, prevent fraud, or protect the personal safety of users or the public.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Sokoyuku Limited is based in Hong Kong. As a global platform, we and our service providers (like Stripe) operate servers and systems in various jurisdictions, including the United States and the European Economic Area (EEA). 

If you are accessing the Services from the EEA, UK, or other regions with comprehensive data protection laws, please note that your Personal Data will be transferred to, stored, and processed in Hong Kong and other countries. We ensure that such transfers are made using appropriate safeguards, such as reliance on adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or other legally valid transfer mechanisms.

## 10. Data Retention

We will only retain your Personal Data for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected. 
- **Account Data:** Retained for the lifetime of your account to provide the Services.
- **Financial Data:** Retained for up to 7-10 years (depending on jurisdiction) to comply with accounting, tax, and financial regulations.
- **Messages:** Message text is not kept. Limited operational records are kept for a limited time, by default 30 days.
- **Voice calls:** Call session and signaling records are kept to operate the call. Call audio is not kept. The call session key stays in your browser.

When we no longer have a legitimate business need to process your Personal Data, we will either securely delete it or anonymise it.

## 11. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your Personal Data. Local Messaging Account credentials and local model settings are encrypted before upload. Sokoyuku cannot read them or recover them if you lose your key. Transmission over the internet is not completely secure. Any transmission of Personal Data is at your own risk.

## 12. Your Rights Regarding Personal Data

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR or UK DPA), you may have the following rights:
- **Right to Access:** Request a copy of the Personal Data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure (Deletion):** Request the deletion of your Personal Data where there is no legitimate reason for us to continue processing it.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Ask us to suspend the processing of your data.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Request the transfer of your data to you or a third party in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 17. We may require specific information from you to help us confirm your identity before processing your request.

## 13. Cookies

We use a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in. It stays on your device until it expires or you sign out. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or web beacons. Language, theme, and a voice-call session key are stored in local storage, not in a cookie. Stripe may set cookies during checkout. Google or Telegram may set cookies when you use their sign-in button. Sign-up and password change load Cloudflare Turnstile.

For detailed information about the types of cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control your cookie preferences, please review our separate [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).
